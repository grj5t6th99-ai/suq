Where the money goes

Goods (FOB) Freight & insurance Customs duty Excise tax Clearing, inland, demurrage
Duty is computed on CIF (FOB + apportioned freight and insurance). Excise is paid at import and capitalised into stock — it never appears as a line on a customer receipt. Rates shown are defaults: GCC-origin goods and most basic foodstuffs are entered at 0%, other goods at the 5% common external tariff. Confirm every HS code and the excise valuation basis with the General Tax Authority and your clearing agent before relying on these figures.

Receiving gate GRN-2026-0891 · Al Wakra DC

75%
Qatar imports almost everything, so shelf life is consumed in transit before the goods reach the shelf. The gate is applied at receipt, not at the shelf: a pallet that arrives with too little life left is a supplier claim today and a write-off in six weeks. Chilled lines also fail on temperature, and meat and poultry fail on an expired halal certificate regardless of condition.

Stock cover & expiry

Lead times are route-dependent: local 2 days, Abu Samra land 7 days, air 4 days, Hamad Port sea 28 days. Order-by dates work backwards from cover, which is why Ramadan procurement has to start six weeks early on sea freight. Because Ramadan moves roughly eleven days earlier each Gregorian year, comparatives run on the Hijri calendar — "same week last year" is the wrong baseline in this market.
